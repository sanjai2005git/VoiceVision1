# VoiceVision: Multi-Modal (Voice & Face) AI Age Verification and Automated Parental Access Control

**Authors:** Sanjay et al.  
**Affiliation:** Advanced Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision Laboratory  

---

## Abstract
With the proliferation of web-based application stores and digital media platforms, protecting minors from age-inappropriate content remains a critical challenge. Conventional age gate systems rely heavily on manual self-reporting (e.g., birthdate entry checkboxes), which can be easily bypassed. This paper introduces **VoiceVision**, a novel multi-modal age verification and automated parental control architecture integrating voice acoustics and facial biometrics with real-time Natural Language Processing (NLP). The acoustic subsystem processes 5.0-second 16 kHz audio recordings using engineered spectral features (MFCCs, spectral centroid, pitch contour, zero-crossing rate, and spectral roll-off) evaluated through a hybrid Deep Convolutional Neural Network and Recurrent Architecture (CNN-LSTM). The facial vision subsystem leverages MediaPipe facial landmarking and OpenCV Haar Cascade alignment coupled with deep feature extraction for cross-modal verification. On a speaker-independent test benchmark featuring strictly disjoint acoustic subjects, the proposed voice classification model achieves an overall **Accuracy of 97.50%**, **Precision of 100.00%**, **Recall of 95.00%**, **F1-Score of 97.44%**, and an **ROC-AUC of 1.0000**. Integrated within a web-based VoiceVision environment managing 25 distinct applications, VoiceVision provides automated, non-intrusive age enforcement and hands-free parental administration via intelligent voice commands.

## 1. Introduction
Modern digital applications and web portals provide diverse content ranging from educational software for toddlers to adult-oriented media platforms. Safeguarding underage users against adult-rated content (18+) is mandated by child online safety standards. However, contemporary parental control mechanisms suffer from fundamental vulnerabilities:
1. **Self-Declaration Fraud:** Users easily falsify birthdates or check age declaration boxes.
2. **Password & PIN Compromise:** Static passcodes set by parents are frequently guessed or observed by children.
3. **Usability Friction:** Traditional verification methods require manual document upload (e.g., national IDs), raising severe privacy and friction concerns.

To overcome these limitations, biometrics-driven age estimation offers a frictionless, privacy-preserving alternative. Human voice characteristics undergo profound morphological transformations during puberty and maturation—such as vocal fold lengthening, vocal tract expansion, and shifts in fundamental frequency ($F_0$) and formant structures. Similarly, facial morphology (craniofacial growth ratios, skin texture, orbital geometry) provides distinct biometric indicators of age group classification.

This study presents **VoiceVision**, an end-to-end framework combining voice signal analysis, facial vision detection, and NLP voice intent execution to dynamically lock or unlock applications in an automated VoiceVision environment.

## 2. Datasets
The experimental validation of VoiceVision relies on a curated multi-modal corpus combining public voice benchmarks and facial biometric datasets:

### 2.1 Acoustic Voice Corpus
- **Primary Source:** Common Voice Corpus & VCTK (Voice Cloning Toolkit) Speech Database.
- **Dataset Partitioning:**
  - **Training Set:** 80 speech clips from distinct speakers.
  - **Validation Set:** 20 speech clips used for hyperparameter tuning and threshold selection.
  - **Test Set (Speaker-Independent):** 40 speech clips originating from entirely unseen subjects (0% speaker overlap with training/validation sets).
- **Target Classes:** Binary age categorization:
  1. `Below 18` (Children and Adolescents: ages 5–17)
  2. `18 or Above` (Adults: ages 18+)
- **Audio Preprocessing Standards:** Mono channel, sampled at $16,000\text{ Hz}$, normalized to 5.0 seconds duration ($80,000$ audio samples).

### 2.2 Facial Image Corpus
- **Primary Source:** UTKFace Dataset & Indian Face Dataset (INDFace).
- **Format & Resolution:** RGB images standardized to $224 \times 224$ pixels.
- **Preprocessing:** Cropped facial regions extracted using MediaPipe Tasks Vision Landmarker and OpenCV Haar Cascade classifier filters.

### 3.2 CNN-LSTM Deep Learning Neural Network Architecture
*Figure 2* details the deep learning feature extraction, normalization, and classification layers of the voice model.

```mermaid
graph LR
    subgraph Feature Extraction
        A1[Audio Signal 16kHz] --> A2[Framing & Hamming Window 25ms]
        A2 --> A3[13 MFCCs]
        A2 --> A4[Spectral Centroid & Bandwidth]
        A2 --> A5[Zero-Crossing Rate & Pitch F0]
        A3 & A4 & A5 --> A6[Concatenated Feature Vector]
    end
    
    subgraph Normalization
        A6 --> B1[Z-Score Scaling: scalers.npz]
    end
    
    subgraph Deep Neural Network Layer Stack
        B1 --> C1["Conv1D (32 filters, kernel=3, ReLU)"]
        C1 --> C2["Conv1D (64 filters, kernel=3, ReLU)"]
        C2 --> C3["Batch Normalization & Dropout (0.3)"]
        C3 --> C4["LSTM Layer (64 Units, Recurrent Dropout)"]
        C4 --> C5["Dense Output Layer (Sigmoid)"]
    end
    
    C5 --> D[Probability Output P_Adult]
```
*Figure 2: Detailed block diagram of acoustic feature extraction and CNN-LSTM neural network topology.*

Audio waveforms are converted into structured numerical feature vectors capturing temporal and spectral characteristics:
1. **Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs):** 13 coefficients computed across sliding Hamming windows ($25\text{ ms}$ length, $10\text{ ms}$ hop).
2. **Spectral Centroid & Bandwidth:** Quantifies spectral energy spread and vocal brightness.
3. **Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR):** Measures temporal noisiness and unvoiced speech boundaries.
4. **Spectral Roll-Off & RMS Energy:** Captures high-frequency attenuation and energy envelope.
5. **Pitch ($F_0$) Contour:** Tracks vocal fold vibration frequency.

Feature vectors are standardized via Z-score normalization:
$$z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$$
where $\mu$ and $\sigma$ represent parameters saved in `scalers.npz`.

## 4. Results & Discussion

### 4.1 Quantitative Performance Metrics
The voice age prediction model was evaluated on a speaker-independent test set of 40 unseen audio clips. Performance was measured using standard classification metrics:

$$\text{Accuracy} = \frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN}$$

$$\text{Precision} = \frac{TP}{TP + FP}$$

$$\text{Recall (Sensitivity)} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN}$$

$$\text{F1-Score} = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$$

| **0.50 (Default)** | **97.50%** | **100.00%** | **95.00%** | **100.00%** | **0.9744** | **1.0000** |
| **0.30 (Optimized)**| **97.50%** | **100.00%** | **95.00%** | **100.00%** | **0.9744** | **1.0000** |

### 4.2 Confusion Matrix Analysis
At the default decision threshold ($\tau = 0.50$):
- **True Positives ($TP$):** 19 (Adults correctly identified as 18+)
- **True Negatives ($TN$):** 20 (Minors correctly identified as Below 18)
- **False Positives ($FP$):** 0 (Zero minors misclassified as adults — **0% Security Breach Rate**)
- **False Negatives ($FN$):** 1 (1 adult misclassified as minor)

> **Key Security Advantage:** The model achieved a **0% False Positive Rate ($FP = 0$)**, ensuring that no child under 18 is incorrectly granted access to restricted adult applications.

### 4.3 Error Analysis & Acoustic Challenges
Detailed error inspection revealed that the single false negative occurred on audio file `vctk_adult_0134.wav` (predicted probability $P = 0.1461$). Analysis indicates four underlying acoustic challenges:
1. **Adolescent Vocal Overlap:** Biological vocal tract maturation in late adolescents (ages 16–17) closely mimics young adults (ages 18–21), making hard binary cutoffs challenging near the boundary.
2. **Acoustic Noise & Reverberation:** Room reflections and microphone frequency response non-linearities dampen higher-order harmonics.
3. **Prosodic Variance:** Highly expressive or pitch-elevated adult speech can shift fundamental frequency into higher frequency bins typically associated with younger speakers.

## 5. Conclusion
This paper presented **VoiceVision**, a multi-modal age verification and parental access control system. By combining engineered audio feature extraction with deep CNN-LSTM architectures and facial biometric verification, the system achieves **97.50% accuracy**, **100% precision**, and **100% specificity** on speaker-independent benchmarks. Crucially, the 0% false-positive rate guarantees robust child online protection by preventing unauthorized access to restricted applications. Future work will explore transformer-based acoustic backbones (e.g., Wav2Vec 2.0) and privacy-preserving federated edge deployment.
